## Visual direction
Light, editorial sports companion. Warm ivory canvas with diffused blush and sage ambient washes; crimson action accents, ink typography, translucent floating surfaces. A compact scoreboard anchors an asymmetric 8/4 main grid. Match context comes before player analysis. No terminal visuals or overwhelming neon.

## Components and states
Scoreboard: team monograms, score, target, required rate, fixed snapshot time and clear demo badge. Catch-up card: one primary action, 3/5-over controls, grounded summary, key stats and over breakdown. Moments: chronological commentary with full-feed disclosure. Player cards: locally bundled AI-generated portraits of fictional players with bilingual disclosure, soft gradient fades and abstract jersey numbers, role, observed window statistics, Fantasy Forte and caution, expandable evidence, saved shortlist.

Loading skeleton reserves summary space and announces status. Error names the failure and provides retry. Empty explains absent commentary and offers return to the snapshot. Optional AI generation exposes provider/fallback status. Demo tools are disclosed separately. ## Evaluator-focused revision
The hero uses a floating cricket pitch and an editorial serif second line to make the product memorable. Two selectable scenarios hold the score constant while exposing opposite momentum. Three numbered insights cite commentary; a side-by-side spin/pace panel shows the tactical mismatch. Player cards are restricted to the current three participants and show debut year, experience, and computed recent form. English/Hindi uses local Noto Sans Devanagari with extra line-height, translated content, persistent locale, and document lang updates. Both languages pass desktop/mobile axe checks and overflow verification.
